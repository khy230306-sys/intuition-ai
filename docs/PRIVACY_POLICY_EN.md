## 1. Personal Data Collected

LottoLens does **not** collect personal data. There is no sign-up, login, or payment.

Not collected:

- Name, email, phone number
- Location, contacts
- Photos, user IDs, device IDs
- Advertising identifiers, tracking data, purchase information

## 2. Camera and Photos

This app does **not** use the camera or photo library.

## 3. On-device Processing

- Historical lottery draw data used for statistics is **bundled** in the app and processed on device.
- Numbers selected by the user are used only for on-screen analysis and are not uploaded to a server.
- There is no account database.

## 4. Third-party Services

- The app does not send personal data to external APIs at runtime.
- Google Fonts may load when a network is available for typography only. Core features work offline.

## 5. Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties.

## 6. Retention

No personal data is collected, so no retention period applies.

## 7. Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected, access/correction/deletion requests do not apply. Contact us with app questions using the contact above.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will notify users via the app or an official channel.

## 9. Note

LottoLens is a statistics and entertainment analysis tool. It does not sell, buy, or process lottery tickets or gambling payments.
